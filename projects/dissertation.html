LLM-Driven RPG Game

Dissertation project — University of Exeter, First Class.

A Unity RPG where NPCs are powered by an LLM instead of a fixed dialogue tree: they remember past conversations with the player and generate dynamic quests on the fly, rather than following a scripted list.

More on the architecture, the memory system, and how the quest generation pipeline works is coming soon.
